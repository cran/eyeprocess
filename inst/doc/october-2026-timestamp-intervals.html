Reported sampling rates and observed timestamp intervals

Measurement question

Device-reported rate, source-export timestamp intervals, frame-delivery cadence, and downstream resampling frequency are distinct quantities. A declared sampling frequency should never be substituted for empirical timestamp checks; neither observed intervals nor a device specification by themselves establish synchronization accuracy, calibration quality, or the temporal accuracy of a fixation or pupil event.

This deliberately synthetic example begins with a nominal 120 Hz grid, introduces jitter, and inserts a few delayed intervals to show why mean realized rate and interval distribution should be reported separately.

set.seed(20261022)
n <- 501L
nominal_ms <- 1000 / 120
interval_ms <- pmax(0.1, stats::rnorm(n - 1L, nominal_ms, 0.55))
delays <- seq(95L, 395L, by = 60L)
interval_ms[delays] <- interval_ms[delays] * 2.6
timestamp_ms <- c(0, cumsum(interval_ms))
realized_rate_hz <- (length(timestamp_ms) - 1L) * 1000 /
  (tail(timestamp_ms, 1L) - timestamp_ms[1L])
stopifnot(length(timestamp_ms) == n,
          all(diff(timestamp_ms) > 0),
          realized_rate_hz < 120)
data.frame(declared_hz = 120, observed_mean_hz = realized_rate_hz,
           interval_q05_ms = unname(stats::quantile(interval_ms, 0.05)),
           interval_median_ms = unname(stats::median(interval_ms)),
           interval_q95_ms = unname(stats::quantile(interval_ms, 0.95)))
##   declared_hz observed_mean_hz interval_q05_ms interval_median_ms
## 1         120         117.5595        7.505306           8.339749
##   interval_q95_ms
## 1        9.276689

Reporting constraints

This demonstration supports an elementary acquisition audit: retain native export timestamps and source rate declarations; calculate realized intervals before interpolation, smoothing or trial aggregation; record clock changes and delayed intervals; and distinguish observed missing records from true absence of the underlying behavioral event. It does not establish whether delayed samples reflect buffering, device loss, timestamp origin, USB delivery, a host computer, or another cause. That inference needs independent reference timing and vendor-specific documentation.
